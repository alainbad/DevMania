DevSmart Calculator

Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026. DevMania publishes DevSmart Calculator.

Calculations and images

The app processes photos and recognizes text on your device. DevMania does not receive your photos, recognized text, calculations, or calculation history. You can use the app without an account, including while offline.

Google ML Kit

The app uses Google ML Kit for text recognition. When a connection is available, the SDK may send Google information such as device and app details, per-installation identifiers, timing measurements, image format and resolution, and event and error details for diagnostics and usage analytics. The text recognition inputs and results are processed on your device. Read Google ML Kit’s terms and privacy information and its iOS data disclosure.

Permissions and storage

Camera access lets you photograph numbers and equations. The photo picker lets you choose an image. The app keeps calculation history and settings on your device. To clear calculations, open History and choose Delete history. Temporary image copies may remain in the device cache. Copying a result places it on your system clipboard.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact DevMania support. If you email us, we use the information you send to respond to your request.
