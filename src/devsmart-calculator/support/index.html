Support

For help with DevSmart Calculator, email DevMania support.

Scan numbers or an equation

Open Camera to take a photo or choose Gallery for an image saved on your device. Choose Numbers for a list of values or Equation for an arithmetic expression. Review and correct the recognized values before calculating. Use Reset & retake to start a new scan.

Fix a scan that didn’t work

Try a sharp photo in good light and frame only the text you want to scan. If camera access is off, enable it for Smart Calculator in your device settings or import a photo through Gallery. A photo stored only in iCloud may need to be downloaded first.

History and appearance

Open History to reuse a result, change Appearance, or delete saved calculations.

Report a problem

Include your device model, OS and app versions, and the steps that led to the issue. Use an example with dummy values if you need to share an image.
