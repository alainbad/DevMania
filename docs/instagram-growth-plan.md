## Four week editorial test
Publish three original reels and one useful carousel per week. Share each to Stories with a simple question. This cadence is a starting experiment, not an algorithm requirement.

Reel structure: show the finished result immediately, demonstrate one useful interaction, explain one design choice, close with one specific reason to follow. Use English captions and readable type. ## Distribution
Reply thoughtfully to comments. Participate in relevant local business and design discussions with useful responses. Invite willing collaborators to co-create a post; get consent before using client logos or naming private projects. No automated DMs, bulk comments or follow/unfollow campaigns.

## Measurement every seven days
Track followers at start/end, accounts reached, profile visits, new follows, watch time, shares and saves for each reel. Compare new follows/profile visits when both counts are available. Compare each reel's follows/accounts reached, using consistent time windows and account-provided metrics.

Website GA4 event: instagram_click with placement and page_path. This measures clicks to the profile, NOT completed follows.
Follower gap = max(0,1000-current followers).
After four weeks, estimate time using the observed net weekly growth, and present a range. Do not invent a baseline or promise a date.
Change one variable at a time (hook, topic or duration) on the next three posts. Keep the best-performing topic and improve the weakest opening.

## Search visibility work
Instagram discovery and Google SEO are separate channels. Improve existing useful service and project pages, connect Search Console, then prioritize pages based on actual impressions, clicks and queries. Do not add thin location pages or mass-produced articles just to raise page counts.
